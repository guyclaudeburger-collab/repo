Title: Human Sexuality
---

From an ecological perspective, it is the relationship of sexuality to the environment in all its forms. It is necessary to consider the quality of one's social environment, the affinities felt for a particular individual (or "[energetic](pourquoi-parler)" affinities), the partner's behavior, the image of sexuality conveyed by culture and morality, the acceptance or rejection of the existence of a transcendent dimension, but also [dietary factors](instincto) and their physiological and psychological repercussions.
